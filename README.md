# Awesome Ad Intelligence [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge-flat.svg)](https://awesome.re)

> A curated list of **ad intelligence** and **competitive analysis** tools — ad creative search engines, app market analytics, web traffic intelligence, and AI-powered research platforms.

This list is for **marketers, growth teams, ad ops, and SaaS founders** who need to research what their competitors are advertising, how much traffic and revenue they're earning, and what creative angles work in their category.

Maintained by [AdMapix](https://www.admapix.com) — AI-powered ad intelligence across 200+ countries.

> **Definitions**
>
> - **Ad creative search** — search and browse the actual ad creatives competitors are running on Meta, Google, TikTok, etc.
> - **Web analytics intelligence** — third-party traffic, engagement, and SEO data for any domain
> - **Mobile app analytics** — downloads, revenue, retention, and ASO data for any app on App Store / Google Play
> - **AI ad intelligence** — research agents that orchestrate dozens of API calls and generate structured reports

## Ad Creative Search

> Tools for browsing and searching the actual ad creatives that brands are running across major platforms.

### Free Official Archives

- [Meta Ad Library](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/) — Free official archive of every active and inactive ad on Facebook and Instagram. Best free starting point.
- [Google Ads Transparency Center](https://adstransparency.google.com/) — Free official browser of ads served via Google Search, Display, Shopping, and YouTube.
- [TikTok Creative Center](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/creativecenter/) — Free official browser of top-performing TikTok ads by region, industry, and objective.
- [LinkedIn Ad Library](https://www.linkedin.com/ad-library/) — Free official archive of LinkedIn ads.
- [X Ads Transparency](https://ads.x.com/transparency) — Twitter/X official ad transparency.

### Commercial Platforms

- [AdMapix](https://www.admapix.com) — AI-powered ad intelligence platform indexing creatives across Meta, Google, TikTok, and other channels in 200+ countries; includes deep research agents for competitor analysis. _SimilarWeb / Sensor Tower / AppGrowing alternative._
- [BigSpy](https://bigspy.com/) — Cross-platform ad spy with one of the largest creative databases.
- [AdSpy](https://adspy.com/) — Long-running Facebook & Instagram ad spy tool.
- [Ideoy](https://ideoy.ai) - Remake this week's viral meme videos from one selfie (AI image-to-video, 9:16, freemium).
- [Pathmatics](https://www.pathmatics.com/) — Display, video, and native ad intelligence (now part of Sensor Tower).
- [SpyFu](https://www.spyfu.com/) — PPC and SEO competitor research focused on Google Ads.
- [Minea](https://minea.com/) — Ad spy focused on e-commerce and dropshipping creatives.
- [PowerAdSpy](https://poweradspy.com/) — Ad spy across Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, Native, Google.
- [Anstrex](https://www.anstrex.com/) — Native and push notification ad spy.
- [Foreplay](https://www.foreplay.co/) — Save and organize ad creatives in a swipe file.

## Web Analytics Intelligence

> Third-party traffic, engagement, and SEO data for any website.

- [SimilarWeb](https://www.similarweb.com/) — Web traffic, engagement, conversion funnel, and digital marketing intelligence. Industry standard.
- [Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/) — Competitive SEO + paid ads + content + market analysis.
- [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/) — Backlink and SEO intelligence.
- [SpyFu](https://www.spyfu.com/) — Historic PPC keyword and ad copy data.

## Mobile App Analytics

> Downloads, revenue, ranking, and ASO data for mobile apps.

- [Sensor Tower](https://sensortower.com/) — Mobile app downloads, revenue, ad creative intelligence (Pathmatics).
- [data.ai](https://www.data.ai/) — Mobile app market data (formerly App Annie).
- [AppMagic](https://appmagic.rocks/) — App store analytics with strong free tier.
- [AppFollow](https://appfollow.io/) — App store optimization (ASO) and review monitoring.
- [Apptopia](https://apptopia.com/) — Mobile app market intelligence.
- [MobileAction](https://www.mobileaction.co/) — ASO, market intelligence, and ad spy for mobile.
- [AppGrowing](https://appgrowing.net/) — Mobile ad creative intelligence (China and global).
- [AppTweak](https://www.apptweak.com/) — ASO and app market data.
